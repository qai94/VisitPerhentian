Privacy Policy

How we handle your data and protect your privacy.

⚠️
Educational Project Disclaimer: This website was developed as part of a student assignment (CSC584) for UiTM. It is not a real commercial tourism site. Any data submitted via forms is for demonstration purposes only and is not stored, processed, or shared with any real-world entities.

Last Updated: December 2025

At Pulau Perhentian (Student Project), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you fill out the "Contact Us" form. This information includes:

  • Full Name
  • Contact Number
  • Email Address
  • Gender
  • Referral Source (How you heard about us)
  • Message content

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for the purpose of simulating a response to your enquiry. In a real-world scenario, this data would be used to:

  • Respond to your questions or booking requests.
  • Improve our website and customer service.
  • Send administrative information, such as updates to terms or policies.

3. Data Protection

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. However, please note that as this is a student project, data submitted is not encrypted with enterprise-grade security and should not include sensitive real-world data (e.g., credit card numbers).

4. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. This website does not include third-party advertising.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy or the nature of this project, you may contact the developer using the information provided on the About Developer page.
