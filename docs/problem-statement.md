# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?

Prospect tourist who is wishing to visit Pulau Perhentian, especially first-time visitors looking for extra information regarding the destination, activities, and places to stay.

## What exactly is going wrong?

Visitors may need to consult multiple sources with questionable verification the information regarding the destination and compare activities and also accommodation. A lack of a single introductory guide can make this initial planning process less convenient.

## Why does it matter?

Extra searching can take time and make it harder to form an initial travel plan. A clear guide can help visitors explore their options before contacting accommodation providers or tour operators.

## Problem statement

Prospective tourist to Pulau Perhentian need an accessible overview of the islands, available activities, and accommodation options before planning a trip. When these details are spread across separate sources, visitors may spend extra time finding and comparing relevant information. This can make it harder for first-time visitors to decide what to do and where to stay. VisitPerhentian addresses this planning problem by bringing introductory destination information, activity descriptions, accommodation summaries, and a photo gallery together in one website. The project is an educational static prototype that also demonstrates an enquiry form, without providing live bookings or sending messages to a server.
