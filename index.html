About Perhentian Islands

Nestled along the east coast of Terengganu, the Perhentian Islands are among Malaysia’s most breathtaking island destinations. Crystal-clear waters, vibrant coral gardens, and unspoiled beaches make them a favourite for snorkeling, scuba diving, and marine wildlife encounters.

Perhentian Besar offers serene, resort-style relaxation, while Perhentian Kecil brings lively beach culture and adventure. Together, they create a paradise where nature, tranquility, and island charm come together.
